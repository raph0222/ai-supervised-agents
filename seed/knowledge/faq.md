# Frequently Asked Questions

Informational. Where this document and a binding policy disagree, the binding policy wins.

## Orders

**Can I change or cancel my order?**
Yes, until it ships. Once a tracking number exists the order cannot be changed, cancelled, or
redirected — you would need to return it after delivery.

**Can I add an item to an order I just placed?**
No. Place a second order; if both are still unshipped we will usually combine them and refund
the duplicate shipping.

**Do you offer price adjustments?**
Yes. If an item goes on sale within 7 days of your purchase, we refund the difference. Once
only, and it does not apply to items bought during a sale event.

## Returns and refunds

**How long do I have to return something?**
{{return_window_days}} days from delivery, unused and in its original packaging with the
anti-static bag, brackets and cables included.

**Is return shipping free?**
Free for Gold and Platinum members. Standard members pay $7.95, deducted from the refund. Always
free if the item is faulty, damaged, or the wrong item.

**How long does a refund take?**
5–10 business days to appear on your statement once issued, controlled by your bank.

**Can I get a refund to a different card?**
No. Refunds always go back to the original payment method.

**Can I return a final-sale item?**
No, final sale is final — that covers the end-of-life clearance kits. We can usually offer store
credit at half the item value as a courtesy.

## Exchanges

**Can I exchange for a different product?**
Not directly. Return the item for a refund and place a new order.

**The card does not fit in my case. Can I swap the case?**
Yes, within the same line. The Atlas Mid Tower clears 330mm cards, the Full Tower 400mm — an
even swap either way, no price difference.

**What if the variant I want is out of stock?**
We will backorder it if it restocks within two weeks, or refund you with return shipping
waived.

## Shipping

**When will my order arrive?**
Standard is 3–7 business days after dispatch, Expedited 2, Overnight next business day. Add
1–2 business days for processing.

**Is shipping free?**
Standard shipping is free on orders over $75, otherwise $5.95.

**Do you ship internationally?**
US, Canada, UK and EU. Duties and import VAT are payable on delivery and are set by your
country, not by us. High-value items are domestic only.

**My tracking has not updated in a day.**
Normal — tracking can lag the parcel by 24 hours. If there is no scan for 5 business days, get
in touch.

## Products and compatibility

**Will the Kinetic DDR5 kit work in my board?**
Only in a DDR5 board — DDR5 and DDR4 are not interchangeable and the slots are keyed
differently. Enable the EXPO or XMP profile in BIOS or the kit runs at its JEDEC speed.

**Does the Titan CPU come with a cooler?**
No. At 350W it needs a 280mm liquid cooler or better; the AM5 and LGA1700 brackets are in the
Frostline box.

**How much power supply do I need for the RX-800?**
750W is the practical minimum. The card is 332mm long and 2.8 slots, so check clearance against
your case first.

**Do you test parts before they ship?**
Graphics cards and processors are bench tested and serial-logged before dispatch. Everything
else ships factory sealed.

**How should I store a spare part?**
In its anti-static bag, dry, away from carpet. Do not stack boards without their bags.

## Account and loyalty

**How does the loyalty programme work?**
Silver from your first order, Gold at $500 lifetime spend, Platinum at $2,000. Gold and above
get free shipping both ways and early access to releases.

**Do loyalty points expire?**
No.

**Does Gold status extend my return window?**
No — free return shipping, not extra time. Everyone gets {{return_window_days}} days.
