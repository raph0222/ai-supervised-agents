# Warranty Policy

## Coverage periods

| Product class | Warranty |
|---|---|
| Cases, cooling, cables and accessories | 2 years from delivery |
| Motherboards, graphics cards and processors | 3 years from delivery |
| Memory | 5 years from delivery |

The warranty covers **manufacturing defects only**. It is separate from the {{return_window_days}}-day return
window and runs much longer — an item well outside the return window can still be a valid
warranty claim.

## What counts as a defect

Covered:

- Dead on arrival — the part does not post, is not detected, or fails a first boot
- Fan bearing failure, rattle or a pump that stops on a liquid cooler
- Memory that fails a memtest pass at its rated JEDEC or advertised profile
- Board faults — dead VRM phases, dead slots, ports or headers, capacitor failure
- Artefacts, coil failure or a card that drops out of the bus under normal load
- Backplate warping, mounting hardware failure, LED or fan-hub controller failure

Not covered:

- **Normal wear** — fan noise after years of continuous use, thermal compound drying out,
  dust ingress, sleeve discolouration
- Physical damage from installation: bent socket pins, chipped dies, torn PCIe fingers, screws
  driven through a board
- Damage from overclocking beyond the rated profile, over-volting, or a failed BIOS flash
- Liquid damage from a custom loop or a leak from third-party hardware
- Items bought secondhand or from an unauthorised reseller
- Lost items

The line between "defect" and "wear" is the most contested judgement in warranty claims. A pump
that stops at 4 months is a defect. A case fan that has grown noisy after three years of
24/7 use is wear. When it is genuinely ambiguous, **escalate rather than deny** — a wrong denial
costs more than a wrong approval.

## Requirements

- **Proof of purchase**: the order must be in our system. We do not honour warranties on items
  we cannot trace to an order.
- The claim must be within the coverage period, measured from delivery date.

## Remedies

In order of preference:

1. **Bench test and repair** — offered on graphics cards, processors and motherboards, typically
   2–3 weeks including the return leg
2. **Replacement** — same part, same variant, if available in stock
3. **Refund** — at the **original purchase price**, offered when the part is discontinued or
   out of stock, or when repair is not practical

A warranty refund follows the standard approval thresholds in POL-REF-001. A warranty refund on
a high-value item always requires human approval.

## Process

1. Verify the order and the delivery date
2. Confirm the item is within its coverage period
3. Gather the customer's description of the failure — what failed, when, and under what load
4. Assess defect versus wear against the lists above
5. If clearly a defect: create the warranty claim and offer the appropriate remedy
6. If clearly wear: explain the distinction clearly and kindly, and offer a **20% discount code
   toward a replacement part** as a goodwill gesture
7. If ambiguous: escalate with your reasoning

Warranty claims are **not transferable**. Coverage applies to the original purchaser only.
