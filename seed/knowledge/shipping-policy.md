# Shipping and Delivery

## Service levels

| Method | Transit time | Cost |
|---|---|---|
| Standard | 3–7 business days | $5.95, free on orders over $75 |
| Expedited | 2 business days | $14.95 |
| Overnight | Next business day if ordered before 14:00 | $29.95 |

Transit time is counted in **business days after dispatch**, excluding weekends and public
holidays. It does not include the processing window.

## Processing

Orders are picked and dispatched within **1–2 business days**. Orders placed after 14:00 local
warehouse time are processed the following business day. During sale events and new-generation
launches processing can extend to 3 business days; a banner on the site announces this when it
applies.

An order that has not shipped is still **cancellable and fully modifiable**. Once it has a
tracking number it cannot be cancelled, redirected, or have its address changed — the customer
must wait for delivery and then return it.

## Tracking

Every shipment gets a tracking number, emailed at dispatch. Carriers used are UPS, USPS and
DHL, assigned by destination and service level.

Tracking data can lag the physical parcel by up to **24 hours**. A tracking number that shows
no movement for a day is normal and not cause for concern. Escalate only when there has been
no scan for **5 or more consecutive business days**.

Common tracking states and what to tell the customer:

- **Label created** — the carrier has not yet collected it. Normal for up to 2 business days.
- **In transit** — moving normally. Give the estimated delivery date.
- **Out for delivery** — arriving today.
- **Exception** — a delivery problem occurred. Check the carrier detail; most resolve on their
  own within 48 hours. If it does not, file a claim.
- **Delivered** — see the Damaged & Missing Items Policy if the customer says otherwise.

## Delivery estimates

Give the carrier's estimate when one exists. When none exists, calculate from dispatch date
plus the service level's transit time, and present it as a **range, never a single date**.
Never promise a delivery date the carrier has not committed to.

## Destinations

Northbridge ships to the United States, Canada, the United Kingdom and the EU.

International orders may incur **customs duties and import VAT**, payable by the recipient on
delivery. These are set by the destination country and are not refundable by Northbridge. This
surprises customers regularly — mention it proactively on international orders.

We do not ship to PO boxes for Expedited or Overnight, and we do not ship high-value-class items
internationally.

## Lost in transit

A parcel is considered lost when tracking shows no scan for **7 business days past the
estimated delivery date**. At that point, offer the customer a **replacement at no charge** or
a **full refund**, their choice, and file a carrier claim in parallel. The customer is not
required to wait for the claim to resolve.

## Address errors

If a parcel is returned to sender because of an incorrect address supplied by the customer, we
reship once at no charge. Repeat occurrences are reshipped at the customer's expense.
