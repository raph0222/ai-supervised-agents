# Damaged, Missing and Incorrect Items

## Damaged on arrival

Report window: **14 days from delivery**.

A part that arrives damaged — a cracked PCB, a bent bracket, a dented radiator, a crushed
heatsink — is resolved at **no cost to the customer**: free return shipping,
full refund of item and original shipping, or a free replacement, whichever the customer
prefers.

Do not require the customer to return a damaged item before the replacement or refund is
issued. Damage claims are settled on the customer's description.

Where the damage is cosmetic and minor — a scuffed side panel, a scratched backplate — and the
part works and the customer would rather keep it, offer a
**partial refund of up to 30% of item value** instead. This is often the outcome the customer
prefers and it avoids a return shipment entirely.

Claims above the standard threshold still require approval — a damaged high-value item is still
a high-value refund and still goes to human review.

## Missing package — marked delivered but not received

This is one of the most common contacts and the sequence matters.

1. Ask the customer to **wait 48 hours** from the delivered scan. Carriers frequently mark a
   parcel delivered up to a day before it physically arrives.
2. Ask them to check the usual places — porch, mailbox, side door, building reception, and with
   neighbours.
3. Confirm the delivery address on the order is correct and is where they expected it.
4. If it is still missing after 48 hours, file a claim and offer a **replacement or a full
   refund**, the customer's choice.

Do not make the customer wait for the carrier investigation to conclude. Settle first, claim
in parallel.

**Second occurrence**: a customer reporting a second missing package within 90 days is escalated
for review before any refund or replacement is issued. This is a fraud control, not an
accusation — do not tell the customer they are suspected of anything. Say the claim needs a
short review and set the expectation of a response within one business day.

## Missing item from a multi-item order

Verify what the warehouse recorded as shipped. If the order shipped in **multiple parcels**,
the remaining items may still be in transit with a separate tracking number — check before
treating it as missing.

If a single parcel was short-shipped, ship the missing item immediately at no charge, or refund
that line item if the customer prefers or it is out of stock.

Missing accessories — a mounting bracket, a cable, a driver stick — count under this section.
Ship the accessory on its own; do not ask for the whole part back.

## Wrong item received

Free return shipping and a free reship of the correct item. Do not deduct any fee, and do not
wait for the wrong item to come back before shipping the correct one.

If the correct item is out of stock, offer a full refund including original shipping.

## Empty parcel or visible tampering

Treat as a carrier loss, not a customer claim. Settle with the customer immediately —
replacement or refund — and file a carrier claim with the parcel weight recorded at dispatch.

## Documentation

Every claim under this policy is logged as a CRM ticket with the customer's description, the
resolution offered, and the outcome. Claim history is what makes the second-occurrence rule
above work.
